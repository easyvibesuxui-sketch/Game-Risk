# Sovereign — handoff

Single-player world-conquest game on a painted-atlas board. Classic 42-territory rules, five fantasy
realms. Never use the trademarked board-game name in UI, copy or commits. No auth, no database;
saves are `localStorage` only. The player writes Georgian; the game UI is English.

Live: https://easyvibesuxui-sketch.github.io/Game-Risk/ (Pages, built by `.github/workflows/pages.yml`
on every push to `claude/elegant-cori-akidd3` or `main`; tests run before the build).

## Run

`npm run dev` (Vite on 127.0.0.1:8080) · `npm test` (vitest) · `npm run typecheck` · `npm run build`.

## Rules (do not change while polishing art)

- 42-node graph in `src/game/map.ts`. Bonuses NA 5, SA 2, EU 5, AF 3, AS 7, AU 2.
- Starting armies (`STARTING_ARMIES`): 2→40, 3→35, 4→30, 5→25. One army on each dealt territory; the
  human places the rest as the setup reserve. AI openings are deployed at `create`.
- Attacker 1–3 dice vs defender 1–2 (defender always rolls the most it can), high to high,
  defender wins ties. Occupy moves at least the dice rolled.
- Cards: 42 + 2 wild. Sets: three alike or one of each. Values 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, then +5.
  +2 on a traded card's territory you own. Five or more cards forces a trade. Eliminating a player
  takes their cards; six or more means a forced trade mid-attack (`resumeAttack`).
- One fortify march along a friendly path, then the turn ends.
- Game over when the human owns everything or loses their last land.

## Factions

`src/game/factions.ts`: Humans, Elves, Dwarves, Orcs, Northmen. The player picks one; 1–4 rivals get
the others (so at most 5 players). Portraits/emblems/ornaments are Kling renders in
`public/atlas/mythic/`, reached through `atlasUrl()` so the Pages subpath works.

## Difficulty

`difficulty` lives on the state (easy / medium / hard, picked on the title screen; old saves load
as medium). `PROFILES` in `ai.ts`: easy attacks only at 2.5× odds, one capture a turn, random
drafts, no fortify; hard chases continents, breaks rivals' bonuses and hunts the leader. A medium
AI wins about 60% vs easy, 42% vs medium, 32% vs hard in 3-player self-play. Five easy AIs never
finish on their own (they trade lands back and forth); the test only checks that they keep moving.

## Films

`public/video/intro.*` (all five races on one battlefield; the preloader, plays while the board art
loads) and `victory.*` (when the human conquers the world; the winner's portrait and emblem are laid
over it). Kling 3.0 omni, 10 s, 1080p with native audio, re-encoded to 720p WebM (VP9/Opus) plus MP4
(H.264/AAC). They start muted when the browser blocks autoplay sound and show "Sound on". A film
that cannot play is skipped.

## Board

`src/game/map-shapes.ts` is generated by `scripts/make-map.py` from Natural Earth 110m countries:
countries grouped into territories, Russia/Canada/USA/Australia cut by lon/lat boxes, piecewise
lon/lat stretch into a 1400×860 viewBox. Shared borders come from one partition, so do not simplify
territories one by one. Russia is shifted across the antimeridian before `make_valid`, otherwise
Chukotka smears into horizontal bands. Sea lanes are graph neighbours whose shapes do not touch
(lines that would cross other land are dropped; Alaska–Kamchatka runs off both edges).

Kling textures: `sea.jpg` (engraved waves under the stipple) and `terrain.jpg` (engraved hills and
woods, multiplied over the continent washes through the `#land-only` mask). `cartouche.jpg` frames
the title with an elliptical CSS mask instead of a cut-out.

Owner is shown as a clipped inner border in the owner's colour plus the army seal. Ornaments and
ocean names sit under the `#open-sea` mask so they never paint on land. On phones the board fills
the height and pans sideways.

## AI

`aiStep(state)` returns the next state or `null` (human's turn / game over). The campaign timer
keeps the same reference: `aiStep(current) ?? current`.
